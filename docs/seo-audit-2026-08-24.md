## Open findings, by priority

### High

1. **Every deal's `lastChecked` is now stale.** 45 deals say "Checked June 27, 2026" (58 days) and 155 say "Affiliate datafeed price, June 2026". Nothing is inside the 14-day "current" window, so freshness badges and full reasons are suppressed site-wide — and both cohorts hit the 90-day "expired" cliff in September–October, which strips prices from displayed reasons entirely. **Action (yours):** re-verify prices for at least the 40 indexable deals and update `lastChecked`; consider a standing weekly refresh of the top clicked deals. Do not bulk-bump dates without actually checking — the honesty gate is the product.
2. **GSC still not set up** (carried over from Aug 16, still the highest-leverage manual action). Verify the domain, submit the sitemap, request indexing for the 40 deal URLs + now 54 blog URLs. The new `GOOGLE_SITE_VERIFICATION` env slot makes this a five-minute job.

### Medium

3. **E-E-A-T: no named human anywhere.** All 54 posts say "SulitScan Team"; About/Contact name no person and list no address or business registration. One named author with a short bio (even first-name + role) on the guides would materially help. **Needs your call** on identity/privacy trade-off.
4. **Six oldest guides not reviewed since June 8** (78 days) — includes the evergreen core (how-we-check, Temu guide, fake discounts, voucher checklist, final-price guide). Re-review and bump `lastReviewed` only when actually re-checked.
5. **Newsletter capture is absent from the highest-intent pages** — deals index, category, and store pages have no signup. Consider the compact variant on `/deals` at minimum.
6. **Deal descriptions: 40 of ~217 written.** By design the rest are noindexed, but each description written is an indexable page; prioritize whatever `affiliate_click` events show people actually clicking (Vercel Analytics → Events).

### Low / backlog

7. No automated liveness check for affiliate URLs (`check-links.mjs` is static-only); a monthly manual spot-check of top deals is the cheap fix.
8. Static/deal/category routes omit sitemap `lastModified` (posts have it). Harmless; add if a source of truth per route exists.
9. Per-page OG images for deals could be generated from product images at some point; every blog post now has a unique one, which covers the pages that get shared socially.
