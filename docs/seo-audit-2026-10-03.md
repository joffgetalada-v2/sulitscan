# October growth and freshness audit — 2026-10-03

## Executive summary

The highest-priority problem was freshness and indexability drift, not a shortage of pages. The
catalog had 169 public deal records and **zero active records** at the audit instant. The release
keeps expiry truthful, gives readers guide/store/tool alternatives, aligns the sitemap with page
indexability, and adds five distinct buyer guides. It does not establish ranking or traffic gains.

This document records the October 3 baseline captured in the binding design audit and the local
release implementation. It is not a claim that the changes are already deployed. Source links for
the new collection were rechecked during release preparation. Search Console and analytics results
were not available for this implementation; no search volume, traffic trend, or uplift is invented.

## Measured baseline and priorities

| Priority | Finding and evidence | User/discovery impact | Release action |

| P1 | Pre-fix live sitemap: 157 URLs, including 84 pages emitting noindex and 33 canonical mismatches | Sitemap contradicted page-level indexing signals | Request-time sitemap built from one captured instant; exclude expired deals and empty entity/pagination placeholders |
| P1 | Homepage and unfiltered /deals had zero-inventory dead ends; unfiltered /deals offered a meaningless filter reset | Visitors had little useful onward action | Honest refresh messaging and guide, checkout-tool, and store paths; preserve a distinct filtered-no-result treatment |

| P2 | Blog articles lacked time-based revalidation | Related price cards could outlive eligibility without deployment | Daily article revalidation |
| P2 | Contact handler could treat a resolved provider error as success; non-string input could fail before guarded handling | Users could believe an unsuccessful message was sent | Validate body/field types and lengths, escape controlled text, and handle thrown and resolved provider failures |

The baseline measurements above are preserved evidence, not newly measured post-release metrics.
The sitemap's noindex and canonical counts describe separate checks and must not be summed as
disjoint groups. No Core Web Vitals score or new client-byte reduction is asserted here without a
fresh production measurement.

## Implemented content and on-page work

- Published registry entries post-060 through post-064, dated October 3: food containers, Sephora
  mini/full-size value, mattress protectors, phone tripods, and Christmas lights. The registry now
  contains 64 guides.
- Each has a direct opening answer, separate assessment/limitations/disclosure sections, a worked
  decision, a checkout checklist, six tags, recommendation intent, three concise FAQs, contextual
  internal links, and a unique locally hosted editorial banner.
- Existing article rendering supplies canonical metadata, BlogPosting/Breadcrumb/FAQ JSON-LD,
  visible FAQ controls, and tracked contextual sister-site links. No Product/Offer claims were
  added to the articles.
- ImportTaxPH appears only in the new food-container guide's landed-cost comparison; ApplyReadyCV
  appears only in the new tripod guide's application-material preparation paragraph. No hiring,
  discount, customs, stock, or product-performance outcome is promised.
- Official references are scoped to their actual propositions. US FDA/USDA guidance is identified
  as US guidance. Uratex, IKEA, and Manfrotto specifications apply only to named models. The DTI-BPS
  2021 reminder is historical; the current scope exclusions do not establish safety.
- Banner provenance, exact alt text, progressive JPEG dimensions, and accepted SHA-256 values are
  recorded in the image README and tested. The banners are illustrative, not product-test evidence.

The unfiltered blog remains self-canonical and indexable. Filtered/query views point canonically
to /blog and emit noindex,follow; their ItemList describes only visible results. At the fixed
October 3 zero-active-catalog state, the final sitemap contract is **78 URLs: 14 permanent routes
and 64 blog routes**, with no expired deals, empty entity pages, or query placeholders.

## Verification and release boundaries

The release's executable gates cover fixed historical populated inventory and the current empty
state, recommendations, sitemap policy, contact errors with a mocked provider, campaign boundaries,
blog filtering/metadata, and content/media contracts. Run npm run check plus full Playwright before
promotion; render each new article to check image delivery, FAQ/schema agreement, mobile overflow,
and canonical/indexability. The task report retains the actual command results.

Deployment verification remains separate: inspect the public homepage, /deals, blog filters, all
five articles and images, sitemap membership, canonical/robots/schema, and seasonal promotion after
the owner-authorized promotion. A local build cannot prove that the deployed cache has refreshed.

Fresh release-preparation dependency audit (October 5): `npm audit --audit-level=high`
exited 1 with six high and one critical vulnerability entries. The critical Next.js finding is
GHSA-vcvr-r3jv-pc5j (next/og ImageResponse; installed Next 16.3.4). The audit suggests Next 16.3.8;
high findings also affect brace-expansion and the braces/micromatch/fast-glob ESLint dependency chain.
This is a release blocker for separate dependency review and verification, not a content fix or
a claim that this site's runtime has been exploited. No dependencies were changed in Task 6.

## Owner-dependent follow-ups

1. **Catalog owner:** obtain current partner data, independently check candidates, and publish only
   genuinely refreshed records. Do not bulk-advance lastChecked or weaken expiry to manufacture stock.
2. **Site owner:** authorize promotion, then verify deployed indexing signals and submit or refresh
   the sitemap in the existing Search Console property if appropriate. Monitor exclusion reasons.
3. **Editorial owner:** provide Search Console query/impression exports and existing landing-page
   performance. Use them to identify unanswered decisions and overlap before assigning more guides.
4. **Operations owner:** verify the configured contact provider, domain, aliases, and delivery in an
   approved live test. Automated checks do not send real messages or prove inbox delivery.
5. **Performance owner:** measure deployed client transfer/execution and field Core Web Vitals after
   release. The scanner import change alone does not establish a field-performance improvement.
6. **Commercial/compliance owner:** recheck partner destinations and current terms. No account,
   advertising, consent-platform, publisher-ID, registration, or business-status change is included.

## Sustainable cadence

Recommend **five high-quality source-backed guides per week only when quality holds**. Each needs
a distinct buyer decision, current primary sources, useful internal links, honest limitations, and
reviewable media. Publish fewer when that bar cannot be met; do not produce thin variations to fill
a quota. Prioritise actual Search Console queries and impressions, reader questions, and content
gaps instead of fabricated keyword-volume estimates. Update existing event URLs where the intent
is unchanged. The next research queue and acceptance gate are in content-calendar.md.

Track discovery and outcomes only after a baseline is available: submitted/indexed canonical URLs,
guide query impressions/clicks, guide-to-store/tool navigation, and relevant completion events.
Interpret seasonal changes with their date range and inventory context. This report makes no
promise about rankings, traffic, affiliate revenue, or conversion gains.
