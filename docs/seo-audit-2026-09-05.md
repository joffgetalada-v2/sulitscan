# SulitScan PH — September Growth SEO Audit (September 5, 2026)

This audit records the evidence and bounded decisions behind the September growth release. It is a
repository and public-source audit, not a claim of authenticated search, analytics, affiliate, or
advertising data.

## Audit evidence and current index policy

- The repository exposes 169 active deal records. Exactly 40 have unique editorial descriptions and
  remain eligible for indexing; the other 129 thin records remain browsable with `noindex`. This
  40-deal gate is intentional and should not be widened until a page earns unique, useful content.
- On 2026-09-05 all 169 public listings are in the site's `reference` freshness state. The 45 Shopee
  records last checked on June 27 reach the 90-day expiry threshold on September 26. June affiliate
  datafeed records reach it after September 29. Dates must move only after a real partner-page or
  affiliate-feed verification, never as a bulk freshness edit.
- Before this release, expired direct deal URLs could remain browsable and `noindex` while still
  emitting price-bearing Product/Offer JSON-LD. The release keeps the useful direct page but
  suppresses product schema once freshness is `expired`.
- Sitemap, robots, self-canonicals, BlogPosting and FAQ schema, RSS discovery, and the existing
  unique-content index gate are already generated from repository data. The five new posts increase
  the blog registry from 54 to 59 and are picked up automatically by sitemap and feed generation.

## Competitive content gap

Competing Philippine commerce publishers often lead with named authors, hands-on testing, and
product-specific roundups. SulitScan cannot truthfully claim testing it did not perform or current
availability it did not verify. Its defensible editorial position is narrower and useful: direct
decision answers, source-backed policy distinctions, exact-model evidence checks, worked workflows,
clear limitations, and disclosed affiliate links.

The September cluster addresses five gaps with that approach: marketplace badge meaning, portable
fan format and certification scope, tumbler fit and cleanability, wireless-earbud compatibility and
safe listening, and online foundation shade-matching risk.

## Security and tooling corrections

- Expired deals no longer emit Product/Offer JSON-LD; current and reference deals retain it.
- The audited transitive `browserslist` resolution was moved beyond the affected 4.28.6 release
  without adding a direct runtime dependency.
- ESLint now ignores `.worktrees/**`, preventing generated linked-worktree files from contaminating
  root-checkout lint results.
- The campaign spotlight is isolated in a tested helper with exact UTC boundaries, while the client
  announcement keeps a hydration-safe generic initial state.

## No-account limitations

does not substitute repository counts for live search or commercial performance.
