# SulitScan Competitor Landscape Summary

Snapshot date: 2026-07-23  
Depth: evidence-limited direct-page review

## Scope and evidence rules

This summary records the direct pages reviewed for the July 23 release. It does not claim current
traffic, rankings, prices, coupon validity, cashback rates, inventory depth, or market share. Facts
below describe the observed purpose of the linked pages. Product implications are labeled as
inferences.

## Common observed patterns

Across the direct pages, competitors commonly expose one or more of these search-intent formats:

- broad shopping, comparison, deal, or product-discovery hubs;
- store-specific and coupon-intent pages;
- editorial or verification explanations;
- product comparisons and popularity/discovery pages;
- practical or seasonal shopping guides.

This is a page-pattern observation, not a claim that every competitor provides every format or that
any listed deal, coupon, price, or cashback amount is current.

## Positioning map

The reviewed pages sit on two useful axes:

- **Live commercial-data dependence:** coupon/cashback/price-comparison pages depend heavily on
  current external data; editorial guides depend more on sourcing, clarity, and maintenance.
- **Discovery breadth:** broad hubs cover many shopping intents; store pages and practical guides
  serve a narrower decision.

SulitScan's credible position is narrower and evidence-led: a curated active-deal catalog,
Philippines-specific buying guidance, transparent limitations, and tools that calculate from data the
shopper can verify at checkout.

## Why SulitScan chose a final-checkout comparison tool

**Observed need:** comparison, coupon, store, and deal pages train shoppers to compare commercial
options, while the amount shown before checkout can still differ after shipping, quantity,
discounts, fees, payment offers, or import-cost estimates.

**Product decision:** SulitScan built a user-entered final-checkout comparison tool instead of
presenting unverified live prices or coupons. The calculator:

- compares two final totals and per-unit costs from shopper-entered values;
- runs in the browser and excludes entered values from analytics;
- makes no live monitoring, coupon eligibility, or lowest-price guarantee;
- links to explanatory guidance for checkout changes and possible import costs.

**Inference:** this gives SulitScan a useful comparison surface while staying within its evidence
boundary. It should be judged by calculator usage, guide discovery, and user comprehension—not by
invented parity with live-price or coupon services.

## Opportunities and safeguards

1. Maintain crawlable category/store pagination so broad catalog depth is discoverable.
2. Pair commercial hubs with visible editorial rules, source links, and review dates.
3. Build seasonal guides only where search intent is distinct and the guidance can be maintained.
4. Never describe the checkout tool as a live price monitor or guaranteed savings engine.
5. Do not publish coupon, cashback, popularity, or competitor feature claims without fresh direct
   evidence and a dated verification record.
